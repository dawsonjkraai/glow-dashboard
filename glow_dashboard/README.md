# Glow Dashboard

A touch-first dashboard for wall tablets and phones, in Home Assistant's sidebar. A short setup
builds it from what your home has; then move, resize and swap any card by touch.

- Built for wall tablets, down to a cheap Fire HD 10, and scales to phones and big screens.
- Well over a hundred card styles, full-screen modes (shower, theatre, party, bedtime…), a screensaver, night mode.
- Finds new devices and integrations by itself and adds them where they belong.
- Music across rooms with Music Assistant, live WLED colours, cameras, calendars, lists and more.
